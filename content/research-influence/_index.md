        My coauthored research has been cited and discussed in reports on nurse migration and guidance on artificial intelligence in nursing. These examples document citation and discussion of the work, with links to the original sources.

        ## Nurse migration in a European Parliament commissioned study

        [*The EU legal migration package* (2022)](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/STUD/2022/739031/IPOL_STU%282022%29739031_EN.pdf), commissioned at the request of the European Parliament's Committee on Civil Liberties, Justice and Home Affairs, discusses our [rapid evidence assessment of nurse migration in Australia, Germany and the UK](https://doi.org/10.1177/15271544221102964) (Smith, Herinek, Woodward-Kron and Ewers, 2022).

        On **page 67**, the study uses the review's findings on language, communication and structured integration programmes in its discussion of training and ethical recruitment for migrant long-term care workers.

        ## Citation in the World Health Organization's nursing report

        The World Health Organization's [*State of the world's nursing 2025*](https://www.icn.ch/sites/default/files/2025-05/SOWN%202025.pdf#page=132) includes the same nurse-migration review as **reference 173, on page 112**.

        ## AI ethics in ANA–New York professional guidance

        Jeanine Santelli's [*Artificial Intelligence and Nursing Practice* white paper](https://anany.org/images/Policy%20%20Advocacy/AI%20White%20Paper.pdf), published by ANA–New York on **28 January 2026**, draws on [*An Ethics of Artificial Intelligence for Nursing*](https://doi.org/10.3912/OJIN.Vol30No02Man06), which I coauthored with Jess Dillard-Wright in 2025.

        The white paper cites our work when discussing predictive AI, nurses' responsibility for clinical decisions, changes to nursing work, the sources of patient data, and algorithmic bias (**PDF pages 1, 2 and 5**).
