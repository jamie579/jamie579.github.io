        I am **Dr Jamie Brian Smith**, a nurse researcher and institutional ethnographer. My clinical background in transplant care, critical care and organ donation shapes the questions I ask about nursing work, its value and its conditions.

        I am a **Senior Research Fellow at Edinburgh Napier University**, working on the [SHORTER trial’s process evaluation](https://www.shortertrial.com/our-team), and a **Research Associate at [Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin’s Institute of Gender in Medicine](https://gender.charite.de/en/metas/person/person/address_detail/msc_jamie_b_smith/)**. I also hold an honorary clinical academic role with NHS Lothian.

        My doctoral research at the University of Edinburgh, [*Ecologies of Care*](https://era.ed.ac.uk/items/351c3676-c09d-42e0-b8a1-98a09eb19475), used posthuman institutional ethnography to study nursing work. I continue to work across empirical research and philosophy, often with colleagues in [The Compost Collaborative](https://compostcollaborative.online/people/jamie/).

        I publish as **Jamie B. Smith**, **Jamie B Smith** and **Jamie Smith**. [ORCID 0000-0003-0097-6102](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0097-6102) connects these publishing names.
