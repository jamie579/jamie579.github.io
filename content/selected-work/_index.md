        I design and deliver research on nursing work, clinical interventions and the organisation of care. As a nurse academic, I connect institutional ethnography, critical posthumanism, workforce wellbeing and evaluation. These examples show my contributions to university research, community organisations and creative collaborations.

        ## APPRAISE: alcohol-reduction support for men on remand

        APPRAISE delivered a cross-prison feasibility study of alcohol-reduction support for men on remand, including support after release. The NIHR-funded collaboration recruited **132 men across prisons in Scotland and England** and published its final report in November 2024.

        My work with the University of Edinburgh team spanned the research cycle: conception and design, protocol development, intervention training, data acquisition and drafting the final report. The study generated practical evidence about delivering support in prison and maintaining contact after release, identifying the follow-up arrangements and resources needed for further research. Its findings inform intervention and trial design; effectiveness remains a question for future testing.

        [APPRAISE final report and contribution statement](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/107914/) · [Study protocol summary](/publications/appraise-study-protocol/)

        ## SHORTER: understanding implementation in critical care

        SHORTER compares a five-day antibiotic course with usual-care duration for critically ill adults with sepsis. I work at Edinburgh Napier University as **Senior Research Fellow (Process Evaluation)**, alongside **Process Evaluation Lead Dr Gosha Colquhoun**, within this ongoing UK NHS trial.

        My contributions span study design, protocol refinement and implementation, and co-authorship of the published 2026 trial protocol. My process-evaluation work examines how the intervention is understood and delivered in clinical settings. The team's mixed-methods evaluation connects delivery and fidelity with the organisational conditions shaping practice: how closely delivery follows the protocol, and what shapes departures from it. The evaluation is designed to explain implementation, support interpretation of the trial and inform decisions about future use.

        [SHORTER team and roles](https://www.shortertrial.com/our-team) · [Published trial protocol](/publications/shorter-trial-protocol/)

        ## PflegeStressPlan: nursing stress and working conditions

        At Charité I lead **PflegeStressPlan**, bringing evidence synthesis, quantitative measurement and qualitative inquiry together to investigate nursing stress and the organisation of care. The programme connects questions about working conditions with close examination of how those conditions are measured and experienced. Diary and interview work continues alongside published measurement research.

        I developed the methods, conducted the statistical analysis and led the writing with colleagues for our 2026 study of a 57-item German nursing-stress instrument. Drawing on responses from **440 nursing and midwifery staff in one hospital**, we examined score interpretation and questions that did not apply to some respondents' work. The paper provides provisional support for use in comparable samples and identifies limits on interpreting most subscales separately. The findings give researchers a clearer basis for deciding what their measurements can support before comparing staff or services.

        [Programme leadership and research at Charité](https://gender.charite.de/en/metas/person/person/address_detail/msc_jamie_b_smith/) · [Measurement paper and findings](/publications/nursing-stress-measurement/)

        ## New Nurse Educator: evidence for educational guidance

        My first-author 2023 integrative review informed educational guidance from **A New Agenda for Nurse Educator Education in Europe**, the 2020–2023 Erasmus+ partnership. I contributed through the University of Edinburgh, working with an international team to synthesise evidence on nurse educators' continuing professional development.

        We identified development needs extending beyond teaching competencies to resources, management, collaboration and professional agency. The authors of the partnership's *Empowering Future Nurse Educators* handbook reproduce our model and use the findings in their discussion and recommendations on continuing development, occupational wellbeing and international collaboration.

        [Review summary and citation](/publications/nurse-educator-development/) · [Empowering Future Nurse Educators handbook](https://new-nurse-educator.utu.fi/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Empowering-Future-Nurse-Educators-Handbook-for-nurse-educators2023.pdf)

        ## Community and creative projects

        ### Rivelin Valley Allotments Society

        As a volunteer with Sheffield's **RVAS**, I developed a funding case that translated members' access needs into proposals for a ramp, accessible raised beds and improved shared facilities. I built the Society's website from its records, bringing joining, volunteering, local history and development plans into one resource. I also developed online tool-induction forms and printable safety checklists for members.

        The Society received **£15,890 National Lottery funding in June 2026** for “Improving accessibility and longevity at the allotments”. Members continue to develop the shared space, with further access improvements planned.

        [Access project and continuing plans](https://rvas.co.uk/developments/) · [Tool library and member induction](https://rvas.co.uk/tool-library/)

        ### Steel City Beer & Cider Festival and Blue Bee

        I coordinated the Facebook and Instagram content calendar for Sheffield's 2026 Steel City Beer & Cider Festival as a volunteer. I prepared and scheduled content on the programme, brewers, sponsors and practical visitor information, adapting copy and artwork for each channel and managing accessibility and corrections as plans changed. I also developed and edited a subtitled staff-induction film from volunteer recordings, demonstration footage, narration and festival graphics.

        My homebrew recipe, **A Cup of Ambition**, was among the competition winners selected for commercial brewing. **Blue Bee** brewed it as a New Zealand pale ale for the festival.

        [Festival and volunteer setting](https://sheffield.camra.org.uk/scbf/) · [Homebrew competition and brewing collaborators](https://sheffield.camra.org.uk/2026/10/steel-city-home-brew-competition/)
