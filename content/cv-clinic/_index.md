        ## What it produces

        - a first-draft master academic CV (Word and Markdown);
        - a structured evidence ledger (Excel and CSV);
        - a “How am I known?” positioning summary;
        - targeted suggestions for missing evidence.

        ## Please read first

        Do not upload confidential, patient, participant, student, personnel or private peer-review
        information. Outputs are **working drafts** and must be checked before use. The tool may miss
        things or match the wrong person.

        ## Prefer to fill it in yourself?

        You don’t have to enter anything online. Download a blank template with the same structure and
        complete it offline — no access code needed.
