Academic CV Foundry

Build a useful first draft of your academic CV in about ten minutes.

Add an ORCID, upload an old CV if you have one, answer a few quick questions, and receive a master CV starter and an evidence ledger to review. The tool is built to recover the academic labour that usually falls off a CV — teaching, supervision, peer review, talks, committee work, public engagement — not just to rearrange a publication list.

What it produces

  • a first-draft master academic CV (Word and Markdown);
  • a structured evidence ledger (Excel and CSV);
  • a “How am I known?” positioning summary;
  • targeted suggestions for missing evidence.

Please read first

Do not upload confidential, patient, participant, student, personnel or private peer-review information. Outputs are working drafts and must be checked before use. The tool may miss things or match the wrong person.
