## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: full-colour sci-fi landscape for the About page of a researcher's personal website. Create a distinct companion scene to a dreamy psychedelic space landscape, with a retro illustrated science-fiction feel. Scene: a turquoise alien sea winding between soaring violet and coral rock arches, a large softly luminous ringed planet above the horizon, delicate stars, magenta reflections and a few luminous botanical forms near the shore. Dreamlike, expansive and quietly strange. Digital illustration with crisp layered shapes, subtle painterly texture, rich deep indigo shadows, vivid cyan and warm pink light. Broad 3:2 landscape composition that also crops well to a centred square; put the main rock arch and ringed planet near the centre, leave breathing room around them. It should feel like another world in the same vivid universe, visibly different from orange mountain peaks and blue geysers. No people, no text, no letters, no logos, no borders or watermarks. Keep saturated colour; no duotone treatment.
